\lettersection{About Me}
I am an experienced Digital IC Designer with a strong background in RTL design, verification, and silicon validation. Throughout my career, I have worked on complex high-speed digital designs, collaborating with cross-functional teams to ensure high-performance, power-efficient, and reliable solutions.

I thrive in dynamic environments where I can solve challenging technical problems, optimize design processes, and contribute to the development of cutting-edge semiconductor solutions. Passionate about innovation and continuous improvement, I am always eager to enhance workflows, mentor colleagues, and drive high-quality project outcomes.

\lettersection{Why lowRISC?}
I am drawn to lowRISC's pioneering approach to open silicon and your commitment to transparency in hardware design. During my recent PCIe SoC project, we seriously considered integrating your Ibex RISC-V core, which gave me firsthand appreciation for the quality and practical applicability of your work. The OpenTitan project's achievement in delivering the first commercially available open silicon root of trust chip represents exactly the kind of groundbreaking innovation I wish to contribute to. I'm particularly enthusiastic about joining an organization that fosters collaboration across industry partners, as this collaborative model represents the future of silicon innovation. The opportunity to potentially take on tech leadership for key IPs would allow me to leverage my experience while continuing to grow professionally in an environment that values openness and security.

\lettersection{Why Me?}
With 13+ years designing high-speed digital ICs, my SystemVerilog RTL expertise and full chip cycle experience directly align with OpenTitan's needs. My leadership on PCIe SoC projects (25M gates, 20+ clock domains) and CPU system implementation—including I2C and SPI interfaces—mirrors your requirements for IP development. My verification background (UVM, SVA, formal methods) enables seamless collaboration with DV teams on test planning and debugging. Beyond digital IC technical skills, I've successfully implemented CI/CD workflows and collaborated across organizations by interfacing with external contractors and leading digital teams.

\end{cvletter}
